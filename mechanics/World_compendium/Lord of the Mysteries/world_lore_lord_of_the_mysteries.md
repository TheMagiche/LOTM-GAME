# Lord of the Mysteries

A world of gaslight, steam, and hidden gods. In the Fifth Epoch, humanity thrives in a Victorian age of industry and empire — while above the fog, Beyonders climb twenty-two Sequences of forbidden power, churches wage cold wars over divine authority, and every potion drunk is a bargain with madness. The player is (or becomes) one such Beyonder: someone who drank a potion for power and now must act out a role to keep from losing themselves.

**Tone:** VICTORIAN OCCULT, STEAMPUNK, COSMIC MYSTERY, TENSE, WRY.

## 1. WORLD OVERVIEW

### [CHUNK: OVERVIEW] OVERVIEW — Core Premise
The Fifth Epoch. Steam engines, telegraphs, revolvers, and gas lamps define civilized life across the Northern Continent — but the world's true history is written by Beyonders: humans who drink sequence potions distilled from monster characteristics and gain supernatural powers at the price of creeping madness. Twenty-two pathways each run from Sequence 9 up to Sequence 0; every rung upward demands rarer ingredients, stricter self-mastery, and brings the drinker closer to the godlike "Uniqueness" at its apex — or to becoming a rampaging monster.

Above the mundane world lie the Spirit World and the Astral World; beneath it, the corrupting Abyss. Gods are real, organized into orthodox churches that police Beyonder activity through Nighthawks, Mandated Punishers, and Mechanical Hearts. Sealed Artifacts — 0th-to-3rd-grade objects of compressed danger — trade impossible power for inevitable harm. Nothing here is free: every gift corrodes, every secret has a holder, and divination always carries the risk of being divined back.
**Tone:** VICTORIAN OCCULT, STEAMPUNK, COSMIC MYSTERY, TENSE, WRY.

## 2. FACTIONS

Power in the Fifth Epoch is divided among three great classes of faction: the **orthodox churches**, who hold the pathways and police the supernatural; the **secret organizations**, who operate beyond church control; and the **noble and angel families**, whose bloodlines carry Sequence heritage older than any living nation.

### 2a. ORTHODOX CHURCHES

### FACTION — Church of the Evernight Goddess
**Type:** Orthodox Church (Darkness pathway complete; Death & Twilight Giant near-complete; Fool incomplete to Sequence 3)
**Key Members:** Dunn Smith (Tingen Nighthawks captain), Leonard Mitchell (Sleepless); led by a Pope advised by Thirteen Archbishops and Nine High-ranking Deacons
**Stance:** Lawful establishment in the Loen Kingdom.
The Evernight Goddess rules the night, concealment, and quiet mercy. Her church fields the Nighthawks — plainclothes Beyonder teams that investigate supernatural crime, contain out-of-control Beyonders, and seal dangerous artifacts — backed by the Red Gloves, her elite strike force. Nighthawk squads operate in every major Loen city out of an unmarked chantry, holding nightly prayers to resist corruption. Her cathedral seat rests in Winter County. Internal tension: older hardliners want out-of-control Beyonders destroyed on sight, while field captains like Dunn Smith risk everything to bring rookies home alive. The church is also quietly at odds with the God of Combat's flock across the Feysac border.

### FACTION — Inter-Church Politics & Jurisdiction
**Type:** Framework governing the seven orthodox churches
**Key Members:** The Seven Orthodox Churches collectively
**Stance:** Cold war of faiths, punctuated by formal cooperation.
In polytheistic Loen, jurisdiction over Beyonder incidents divides along the victims' beliefs; when multiple gods' followers are involved, whoever holds dominion over the territory takes the case. The churches share a uniform naming convention for Sealed Artifacts and exchange intelligence on Grade 0 and Grade 1 items so code names never collide — each orthodox church holds five to eight Grade 0 artifacts and fewer than four grounded Angels. Beneath the protocol: the Sun, Storms, and Knowledge churches regard one another as open enemies, and every church watches every pathway for weakness. For a freelance Beyonder, this means the wrong miracle in the wrong parish can trigger a jurisdictional tug-of-war over your corpse.

## 3. LOCATIONS

### LOCATION — Northern Continent (Fifth Epoch civilization)
**Type:** Continent
**Status:** Industrializing, heavily populated
The heart of the known world: fog-bound nations of gas lamps, railways, newspapers, and strict class lines, all built atop older epochs' buried secrets. Most campaigns begin here.
**Features:** [Loen Kingdom, Intis Republic, Feysac Empire, Feynapotter Kingdom, Lenburg, Segar, constant sea links to the archipelagos]

### LOCATION — Loen Kingdom (Northern Continent)
**Type:** Nation — constitutional monarchy
**Status:** Prosperous, orderly, surveilled
Victorian superpower of industry, banking, and colonial trade. Three orthodox churches (Evernight, Storms, Steam) share uneasy custody of public order; the crown rules through parliament, and the secret services watch everyone. Beyonder crime is hushed up as "gas explosions" and "rare diseases."
**Aliases:** [the Kingdom]
**Features:** [Backlund, Tingen, universities, royal secret service, Nighthawk chantries, East Chester County fog]
**ConnectedTo:** [Backlund, Tingen]

### LOCATION — Backlund (Loen Kingdom)
**Type:** Capital city
**Status:** Flourishing, politically tense
The sprawl capital: a million souls divided into West Borough aristocracy, factory districts choking on coal smoke, docklands of sailors and smugglers, and ministries where the real empire gets administered. Above the rooftops, church spires compete with mill chimneys; below the streets, sewers hide things the sanitation office denies.
**Aliases:** [the capital]
**Features:** [West Borough, Cherwood Borough, docklands, St. Samuel Cathedral of Evernight, Backlund Bridge, slums]
**ConnectedTo:** [Tingen, Loen Kingdom, Sonia Sea]

### LOCATION — Tingen (Loen Kingdom)
**Type:** City
**Status:** Quiet on the surface
Mid-sized "City of Universities" on the Dorge River, famous for colleges, publishing houses, and mild weather. Home chantry of Captain Dunn Smith's Nighthawk squad — the city's supernatural deaths are few because the squad works hard and writes careful reports.
**Aliases:** [City of Universities]
**Features:** [Khoy University, Nighthawk chantry, St. Selena Cathedral, Dorge River docks, modest middle-class districts]

France-analogue republic of salons, duels, and guillotined kings. **Type:** Capital city
**Status:** Flourishing, decadent
City of light: boulevards, opera houses, and a bohemian quarter where poets starve beautifully. Beneath the glamour run revolutionary cells, foreign agents, and collectors of the occult who pay cash for curios and ask no questions.

Continental colossus of disciplined infantry, wolf-culture mythology, and deep religious observance. Feysac honors strength and oath-keeping; its giant-blooded nobility claim descent from the Twilight Giant. War with Loen or Intis is always one incident away.

The archipelago's beating heart: a harbor of mixed bloods and tongues, fish markets, adventurer bars, and back-room brokers selling maps to wrecks that shouldn't exist. Every third sailor swears an oath to the Storm; every fourth breaks it.

**Type:** Continent

Land of jungles, savannas, and ancient ruins now carved into colonial holdings. Export: gold, exotic goods, and Beyonder materials harvested from creatures no textbook names. The old kingdoms survive inland, keeping pathways the churches have never catalogued.

### LOCATION — Spirit World (overlay dimension)
**Type:** Parallel realm
**Status:** Alien, layered
A gray-blue expanse completely overlapping reality, inhabited by spirits of varying sanity and appetite. Rituals reach through it; divinations listen to it; travelers without protection are lost in it. Time and distance misbehave.

### LOCATION — Astral World (highest plane)
**Type:** Parallel realm
**Status:** Divine territory
Where gods and high-sequence existences reside, symbolized as radiant spheres above the Spirit World. Prayer, authority, and true miracles originate here. Mortal visitors arrive only by invitation or catastrophe.

**Goals:** To protect what remains of his family, keep his multiple identities alive, and reach the end of the Fool's pathway without becoming something that no longer remembers being human.

## 5. POWER SYSTEM & RULES

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Beyonder Potions & the 22 Pathways
Anyone who drinks a Beyonder potion becomes a Beyonder. Each potion is brewed from main ingredients (a monster's characteristics or flesh) plus supplementary ingredients, following a formula owned by churches, secret orders, or dead families. Twenty-two pathways exist, each a ladder of ten Sequences: 9 (weakest) to 0 (True God). Drinking a higher Sequence's potion grants that Sequence's abilities wholesale — enhanced physique, spirituality, senses, and pathway-specific powers — but each step demands rarer ingredients and brings stronger madness.
**Formula ownership:** churches ration potions to vetted members; secret orders and old families guard their own. Formulas leak, and leaks cause wars.
<!-- rag: always, priority:9 -->

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Acting Method & Digestion
A potion is not power until digested. Digestion advances by living the Sequence's Acting Method — a behavioral creed matching the Sequence's nature (a Seer respects fate; a Clown hides pain behind smiles; a Marauder takes what isn't nailed down). Beyonders who ignore their Acting Method stall at their current Sequence indefinitely. There is no shortcut: advancement is performance, therapy, and penance in one. GM guidance: advancing a Sequence should require obtaining the formula, brewing or buying the potion, drinking through its backlash, and weeks of acting in-character.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:8, triggers: acting method, digestion, advance, potion, formula -->

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Losing Control & Madness
Every Beyonder carries the potion's monstrous nature. Stress, overuse of power, or acting against the Acting Method lets it surface: first as urges and sensory distortions, finally as full Loss of Control — the body warps toward the pathway's mythical-creature form and the mind drowns. Out-of-control Beyonders rampage until killed or sealed; churches exterminate them on sight. Higher Sequences face stronger pressure and stranger temptations (mythical-creature forms, mythification). This is the system's core cost: power that is literally a controlled descent.
<!-- rag: always, priority:9 -->

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Ritual Magic & Divination
Beyonders with spirituality can perform ritualistic magic: chalk circles, symbolic offerings, incantations addressed to beings capable of granting the effect — gods, angels, or oneself-under-a-title. Divination (dowsing, numerology, astrology, dream-walking) reads past, present, and probability, but every act of divination risks alerting what is watched: divining a god is suicide, divinating a rival an invitation. All ritual draws on Spirituality, a personal reserve that regenerates slowly and is consumed by every working.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:8, triggers: ritual, divination, ceremony, prayer, spirituality -->

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Sealed Artifacts & Beyonder Items
Sealed Artifacts are objects containing compressed Beyonder characteristics, graded 3 (dangerous) through 0 (calamitous; effectively a god's fragment). Each grants remarkable powers and inflicts a characteristic downside — madness, curse, compulsion — that grows with grade. Churches seal them in warded vaults under continuous prayer; using one without preparation is a gamble with your mind. Lower-risk items include Beyonder weapons (runed arms with single powers), mystical items, and raw Beyonder characteristics used in crafting or brewing.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:8, triggers: sealed artifact, grade 0, relic, cursed item, beyonder weapon -->

## 6. ECONOMY

### ECONOMY — Currency & Trade
The gold pound dominates: paper currency guaranteed by gold (1 pound = 20 soli = 240 pence). One pound feeds a laborer's family for a week; two pounds buy a good revolver; five hundred put a modest house in a respectable district. Conversions: 1 pound = 24 verl d'or (Intis) = 5.5 gold hoern (Feysac) = 12 risot (Feynapotter). Real rarities trade outside currency: Beyonder characteristics, formulas, sealed artifacts, and diary pages change hands through broker networks where price means "what the buyer can't afford not to pay." Colonial goods (spices, sugar, exotic monster parts) flow north through the archipelagos.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:6, triggers: money, pound, soli, price, market, reward -->

## 7. EVENTS

### EVENT — The Cataclysm and the End of the Third Epoch
The gods' war that shattered the old world: sun blotted, continents sundered, the Eastern Continent forsaken. Modern churches date their founding to the aftermath and guard the era's records jealously. Whatever truly happened is the deepest layer of every conspiracy.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:5, triggers: cataclysm, epoch, history, ancient war -->

### EVENT — Roselle's Industrial Century
Emperor Roselle Gustav introduced steam engines, printing advances, and modern statecraft before his assassination — dragging the world into its Victorian present. His Chinese-script diary, scattered page by page, documents powers and truths no church wants published. Collectors, churches, and secret orders still duel quietly over every recovered page.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:6, triggers: roselle, steam engine, diary, emperor, invention -->

### EVENT — The Antigonus Family's Fall
The Antigonus family, holders of Fool-lineage heritage, were purged and scattered generations ago; their relics — a notebook among them — surface periodically, each surfacing leaving madmen and corpses. The Secret Order exists largely to reclaim what was lost.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:5, triggers: antigonus, notebook, inheritance, purge -->

### EVENT — Current Tensions: Churches, Empires, and the Fog
Loen, Intis, and Feysac maneuver from cold trade wars toward open conflict; the churches jockey for pathway dominance; and in the gray fog above the spirit world, a new Tarot Club convenes weekly under a god who does not officially exist. Rumor places extraordinary Beyonder activity in Tingen and Backlund — disappearances, sealed-artifact thefts, and missionaries of unknown gods.

## 8. BEYONDER MATERIALS & MONSTERS

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Monster Characteristics & Ingredients
Beyonder characteristics crystallize from powerful creatures and dead Beyonders; they are the main ingredient of every potion and the core of sealed artifacts. Hunting monsters is therefore an economy: contracts list species (Lavos Squid, Hornacis mountain goat, Human-Faced Spider), regions, and bounties. Characteristics carry the creature's nature — brew carelessly and the potion's madness reflects the source. Supplementary ingredients (rare herbs, mineral powders, essences) stabilize the brew; substitution degrades safety.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:7, triggers: ingredients, characteristics, monster, hunting, brewing -->
