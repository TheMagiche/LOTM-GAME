# Lord of the Mysteries

A world of gaslight, steam, and hidden gods. In the Fifth Epoch, humanity thrives in a Victorian age of industry and empire — while above the fog, Beyonders climb twenty-two Sequences of forbidden power, churches wage cold wars over divine authority, and every potion drunk is a bargain with madness. The player is (or becomes) one such Beyonder: someone who drank a potion for power and now must act out a role to keep from losing themselves.

**Tone:** VICTORIAN OCCULT, STEAMPUNK, COSMIC MYSTERY, TENSE, WRY.

## 1. WORLD OVERVIEW

### [CHUNK: OVERVIEW] OVERVIEW — Core Premise
The Fifth Epoch. Steam engines, telegraphs, revolvers, and gas lamps define civilized life across the Northern Continent — but the world's true history is written by Beyonders: humans who drink sequence potions distilled from monster characteristics and gain supernatural powers at the price of creeping madness. Twenty-two pathways each run from Sequence 9 up to Sequence 0; every rung upward demands rarer ingredients, stricter self-mastery, and brings the drinker closer to the godlike "Uniqueness" at its apex — or to becoming a rampaging monster.

Above the mundane world lie the Spirit World and the Astral World; beneath it, the corrupting Abyss. Gods are real, organized into orthodox churches that police Beyonder activity through Nighthawks, Mandated Punishers, and Mechanical Hearts. Sealed Artifacts — 0th-to-3rd-grade objects of compressed danger — trade impossible power for inevitable harm. Nothing here is free: every gift corrodes, every secret has a holder, and divination always carries the risk of being divined back.
**Tone:** VICTORIAN OCCULT, STEAMPUNK, COSMIC MYSTERY, TENSE, WRY.

**Stance:** Lawful establishment in the Loen Kingdom.

The church is also quietly at odds with the God of Combat's flock across the Feysac border.

## 5. POWER SYSTEM & RULES

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Beyonder Potions & the 22 Pathways
Anyone who drinks a Beyonder potion becomes a Beyonder. Each potion is brewed from main ingredients (a monster's characteristics or flesh) plus supplementary ingredients, following a formula owned by churches, secret orders, or dead families. Twenty-two pathways exist, each a ladder of ten Sequences: 9 (weakest) to 0 (True God). Drinking a higher Sequence's potion grants that Sequence's abilities wholesale — enhanced physique, spirituality, senses, and pathway-specific powers — but each step demands rarer ingredients and brings stronger madness.
**Formula ownership:** churches ration potions to vetted members; secret orders and old families guard their own. Formulas leak, and leaks cause wars.
<!-- rag: always, priority:9 -->

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Acting Method & Digestion
A potion is not power until digested. Digestion advances by living the Sequence's Acting Method — a behavioral creed matching the Sequence's nature (a Seer respects fate; a Clown hides pain behind smiles; a Marauder takes what isn't nailed down). Beyonders who ignore their Acting Method stall at their current Sequence indefinitely. There is no shortcut: advancement is performance, therapy, and penance in one. GM guidance: advancing a Sequence should require obtaining the formula, brewing or buying the potion, drinking through its backlash, and weeks of acting in-character.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:8, triggers: acting method, digestion, advance, potion, formula -->

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Losing Control & Madness
Every Beyonder carries the potion's monstrous nature. Stress, overuse of power, or acting against the Acting Method lets it surface: first as urges and sensory distortions, finally as full Loss of Control — the body warps toward the pathway's mythical-creature form and the mind drowns. Out-of-control Beyonders rampage until killed or sealed; churches exterminate them on sight. Higher Sequences face stronger pressure and stranger temptations (mythical-creature forms, mythification). This is the system's core cost: power that is literally a controlled descent.
<!-- rag: always, priority:9 -->

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Ritual Magic & Divination
Beyonders with spirituality can perform ritualistic magic: chalk circles, symbolic offerings, incantations addressed to beings capable of granting the effect — gods, angels, or oneself-under-a-title. Divination (dowsing, numerology, astrology, dream-walking) reads past, present, and probability, but every act of divination risks alerting what is watched: divining a god is suicide, divinating a rival an invitation. All ritual draws on Spirituality, a personal reserve that regenerates slowly and is consumed by every working.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:8, triggers: ritual, divination, ceremony, prayer, spirituality -->

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Sealed Artifacts & Beyonder Items
Sealed Artifacts are objects containing compressed Beyonder characteristics, graded 3 (dangerous) through 0 (calamitous; effectively a god's fragment). Each grants remarkable powers and inflicts a characteristic downside — madness, curse, compulsion — that grows with grade. Churches seal them in warded vaults under continuous prayer; using one without preparation is a gamble with your mind. Lower-risk items include Beyonder weapons (runed arms with single powers), mystical items, and raw Beyonder characteristics used in crafting or brewing.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:8, triggers: sealed artifact, grade 0, relic, cursed item, beyonder weapon -->

## 6. ECONOMY

### ECONOMY — Currency & Trade
The gold pound dominates: paper currency guaranteed by gold (1 pound = 20 soli = 240 pence). One pound feeds a laborer's family for a week; two pounds buy a good revolver; five hundred put a modest house in a respectable district. Conversions: 1 pound = 24 verl d'or (Intis) = 5.5 gold hoern (Feysac) = 12 risot (Feynapotter). Real rarities trade outside currency: Beyonder characteristics, formulas, sealed artifacts, and diary pages change hands through broker networks where price means "what the buyer can't afford not to pay." Colonial goods (spices, sugar, exotic monster parts) flow north through the archipelagos.

## 8. BEYONDER MATERIALS & MONSTERS

### [CHUNK: POWER] POWER_SYSTEM — Monster Characteristics & Ingredients
Beyonder characteristics crystallize from powerful creatures and dead Beyonders; they are the main ingredient of every potion and the core of sealed artifacts. Hunting monsters is therefore an economy: contracts list species (Lavos Squid, Hornacis mountain goat, Human-Faced Spider), regions, and bounties. Characteristics carry the creature's nature — brew carelessly and the potion's madness reflects the source. Supplementary ingredients (rare herbs, mineral powders, essences) stabilize the brew; substitution degrades safety.
<!-- rag: keyword, priority:7, triggers: ingredients, characteristics, monster, hunting, brewing -->
