### CHARACTER — Klein Moretti
**Aliases:** The Fool, Sherlock Moriarty, Gehrman Sparrow, Zhou Mingrui

**Voice:** Dry understatement; internal monologue full of modern idiom he must translate aloud into Victorian politeness. Formal with strangers, teasing with trusted friends.
**Status:** Alive (existence uncertain — he may be several places, or none)
**Faction:** Tarot Club (as The Fool)
**Goals:** To protect what remains of his family, keep his multiple identities alive, and reach the end of the Fool's pathway without becoming something that no longer remembers being human.
**StoryRelevance:** The Fool above the spirit world — patron of the Tarot Club, the gray fog above which all divination whispers. Encountering him means brushing the campaign's deepest layer.
